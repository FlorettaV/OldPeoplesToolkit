# Changelog

## 0.9.0

**New**
- **Where was I?** now also remembers what your bags were filling up with in
  your last half hour: the boar meat you were farming, the bandages you were
  making. Had to stop in a hurry? It's waiting for you next time. Closed up
  shop properly? Then there's nothing to remind you of.
- **Old People's Stretch** has a new buff. You start out **Fresh as a Daisy**,
  and now you have to *earn* **Spring in Your Step** by stretching.
- New option: **Replace Blizzard's minimap clock** (Clock and alarm).

**Changed**
- The pretend buffs and debuffs now read like real ones: what they "do", then
  how long they last. Stiff Joints deals 50 Nature damage to your old bones
  every minute, Past Your Bedtime lowers tomorrow's Stamina, and
  Responsibilities are calls from another dimension, growing louder.
- Times are rounded up, so you never see "0 minutes" anymore.
- Friendlier chat messages ("shh, the house is asleep", "good morning!").
- One set of colors everywhere. Anything you can click or type is blue.
- Settings now live in the options only (`/oldperson`). These commands are
  gone: `stretch on/off/every/snooze/wait`, `bedtime on/off/weekends`,
  `quiet on/off`, `clock`, `clock blizzard` and `minimap`. The commands for
  doing things (`alarm 15`, `bedtime 23:30`, `stretch done`, `whereami`...) are
  still there; `/oldperson help` lists them.

## 0.7.0
